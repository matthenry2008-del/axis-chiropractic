Honest answers, before you book

Does an adjustment hurt?

Most people feel relief, not pain. Adjustments are specific and controlled. Many patients look forward to them.

Am I signing up for life?

No. You'll get a clear plan with a defined goal. How long you continue is always your call.

Do I need a referral?

No referral needed. You can book directly, and most new patients are seen the same week.

Is chiropractic safe for kids?

Yes. Care is always adapted to age and size. Dr. Rob has adjusted everyone from babies to great-grandparents.

You'll know what your care costs before we start.

Insurance or self-pay: we'll walk you through it at your first visit, in plain English.

Why it works

Your spine protects the nervous system that runs everything else. When it moves right, your body works right. That's why patients report better sleep, focus, and recovery, not just less pain.
