Lower back pain

The #1 reason people walk in. We find the exact segments driving your pain and adjust those, so you can move, sit, and sleep without bracing against it.

Neck pain & tech neck

Desk life catches up with you. Hours at a screen load the neck and upper back; specific adjustments help restore normal motion and ease the ache.

03

Headaches & migraines

Often a neck problem in disguise. Tension and joint restriction in the neck can trigger recurring headaches; addressing the source can bring lasting relief.

04

Sciatica

Shooting leg pain, numbness, and tingling that travels from the low back down the leg. We work to take pressure off the nerve and calm the symptoms.

05

Sleep & recovery

Wake up rested, recover faster. When your spine and nervous system work the way they should, many patients report deeper sleep and quicker recovery.

06

Kids & pregnancy

Gentle care for growing families. Adjustments are always adapted to age and size, from newborns to expecting moms, with comfort front and center.

07

Car accidents & whiplash

Don't wait: injuries surface later. Even a low-speed collision can strain the neck and spine. Early, specific care helps you heal properly.

08

Sports injuries

Get back in the game faster. From youth sports to weekend warriors, care that supports performance, recovery, and staying in motion.

09

Joint pain & stiffness

Shoulders, hips, knees, arthritis. When joints move better, they tend to feel better; we help restore motion and ease day-to-day stiffness.

10

Pinched nerves & numbness

Tingling in arms, hands, or legs. We look for where a nerve is being irritated and work to relieve the pressure at the source.

11

Posture & scoliosis

Stand taller, move easier. Care aimed at helping your spine function better so posture and movement feel more natural.

12

Work injuries

Lifting, repetitive strain, long shifts. Physical jobs add up over time; specific adjustments help keep you working and feeling well.
