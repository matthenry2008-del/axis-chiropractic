Specific spinal adjustments

What "specific" actually means

Your spine protects the nervous system that runs everything else: sleep, focus, recovery, how you handle stress. A specific adjustment starts with a neurostructural exam to find the exact segments that aren't moving the way they should. Then Dr. Rob adjusts those, and only those.

No one-size-fits-all routine, no cracking everything and hoping. The same care applies whether the patient is a newborn, a linebacker, or a great-grandmother; the technique adapts to the person.
